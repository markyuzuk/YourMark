SENSORIUM RECRUIT

Three tactile, phone-first concepts applying game-design engagement
to clinical trial patient recruitment.

A · TRIALFIT — swipe screener

Replaces the long web intake form. Patients swipe yes/no/not-sure cards; a match ring + confetti make "you qualify" feel like a win. Micro-commitments beat forms.

METRIC → pre-screen completion rate · time-to-interest
B · RESEARCH ROUNDS — daily streak

The wait between "I'm interested" and first site contact is where recruits evaporate. A 15-second daily star game builds streaks, a journey map, and trust.

METRIC → phone-screen show rate · interest→visit conversion
C · RESEARCH HERO — share card

Selfie → hero avatar → branded share card ("I'm exploring a clinical trial"). Personal artifacts turn participants into referral engines.

METRIC → referral clicks · organic reach · cost per enrolled

Learnings ported from Karate Chicken (live game): swipe mechanics, 3-2-1 countdowns, streaks, progress rings, selfie personalization, shareable score cards, recorded-voice rewards.

All three demos run offline, store nothing server-side.
