KARATE CHICKEN

The Official Character Guide

Every challenger who has ever stepped onto the dojo floor, every creature the raiders have thrown at the last student of Mount Coop — catalogued, illustrated, and given the respect (or disrespect) they deserve.

THE HERO

KARATE CHICKEN

The Last Student of Mount Coop

Raised in the dojo rafters and trained in the Way of the Roundhouse Wing. Strikes left, strikes right, misses never. When the raiders come for the grain stores she answers the only way she knows: HI-YAH! Belts: ten. Mercy: none.

THE FOOT SOLDIERS

TURKEY

The Regular

The dojo's most persistent pest — and the most nervous bird in November. Comes from the left, comes from the right, never learns. He knows what season it is, and he knows exactly who the pilgrim brought the carving knife for.

PILGRIM HUNTER

The Bruiser · 2 hearts

Two hearts of pure Thanksgiving appetite. He's not angry at the chicken — he's just very, very hungry, and dinner isn't going to catch itself. Takes two clean hits before he yields. Respect.

FOOTBALL

The Flipper

Thanksgiving means football, and this one spirals in hot. Mid-run it bounces clean across to the other side — the forbidden lateral pass. The red "!" is the only warning you'll get.

GOLDEN TURKEY

The Jackpot · Rare

A turkey dipped in pure jackpot — the prized centerpiece nobody gets to eat. Appears rarely, flees never, and defeating him showers the dojo in points. Legend says he was a normal turkey until he fell into the trophy cabinet.

iPHONE 4 (2011)

Uninvited · 0 hearts

Walks in mid-battle, does absolutely nothing, walks out. Can't run this game, can't run iOS 8, can't hold a call if you hold it wrong. The dojo lets it stay because it's the only combatant older than Caesar's laurel wreath.

THE BOSSES

LUCY

Cavalier King Charles · 5 hearts

A ruby Cavalier in a tactical harness and, for the season, a pilgrim hat. Those silky ears are not decorative — they detect a distracted chicken at forty yards. Five hearts of relentless, cuddle-powered assault. HERE COMES LUCY!

CORN STALKS

Sentient Harvest · 6 hearts

A towering stand of corn with a grudge — harvest season cuts both ways. Golden tassels up top, ears of fury in the husk. Slow, looming, six hearts of fibrous vengeance. Do not shuck.

THE MEATBALL

Rolling Bosses · 2 hearts each · squad of 4 at 10,000 pts

Giant. Rolling. Seasoned. Some say the dojo kitchen produced them during the Great Spaghetti Surplus of '09; others say they willed themselves into existence. Either way: four meatballs roll in at once — two from each side — eight hearts of marinara-fueled menace between them.

THE MEGA MEATBALL

Final Boss · 15 hearts · arrives at 15,000 pts

Twice the size of Karate Chicken herself. The kitchen's magnum opus. It rolls in from one side — take seven hearts off it and it storms back in from the other. MEGA MEATBALL!

THE JOKER

Trickster Boss · 20 hearts · arrives at 20,000 pts

Purple coat, green hair, a grin stretched cheek to cheek. He strolls in asking WHY SO SERIOUS? — then flips sides mid-approach just to keep you honest. Karate Chicken answers the only way she knows how: bat cowl on.

JULIUS CAESAR

Emperor of the Dojo · 7 hearts · fast

Marches in once, fast, at 5,000 points to conquer the dojo, laurel wreath and all. VENI, VIDI, VICI — right up until a certain chicken made him cry ET TU, CHICKEN?!
