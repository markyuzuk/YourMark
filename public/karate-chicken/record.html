🎙 SOUND STUDIO

Record your own sounds for Karate Chicken

How to use:
1. Tap REC next to a sound → make the noise → tap STOP
2. Tap ▶ to hear it. Not happy? Record again.
3. Tap ↓ Download — it saves with the right name automatically.
4. Send me the files — I'll drop them in public/karate-chicken/assets/ and the game uses them instantly.

Tip: keep each sound under 1 second. Say “yum” for yum and a longer “yum yum!” for yumyum. Missing sounds just keep the built-in synth versions.
