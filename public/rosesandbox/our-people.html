Our Leadership Team

Meet the leaders behind the Sensorium Effect.

Matt Kiernan

Founder & CEO

Matt Kiernan is a life sciences entrepreneur who believes culture is the foundation of great companies. Over the past two decades, he has built and exited two successful organizations in the pharmaceutical industry—Pharmica Consulting and Intrinsic Clinical Systems—both acquired by ProPharma Group in 2021.

Matt's approach blends operational rigor with cultural intention. At Pharmica, he launched a company-wide program built around the philosophy "Work hard, have fun, be great," inspiring consultants, clients, and partners to uphold a standard of mutual respect and service. At Intrinsic, he applied that same mindset to technology, creating tools that helped clinical teams work smarter and stay connected.

Now, he's applying that same philosophy to transform clinical research—building a network where investigators, patients, sponsors, and staff all feel supported and valued.

José E. de la Gándara, MD

Medical Director & Principal Investigator

Dr. de la Gándara is a Licensed Physician practicing as an adult and geriatric psychiatrist in the State of Florida for over 30 years. He trained at the University of Florida Shands Teaching Hospital Department of Psychiatry and at the University of Miami Jackson Memorial Hospital Department of Psychiatry Training program, serving as Chief Resident and completing a Fellowship in Psychopharmacology. He directs and serves as Principal Investigator for the Quantum Laboratories Central Nervous System (CNS) Clinical Research program, conducting trials for various CNS indications including Mild Cognitive Impairment, Alzheimer's disease, Depression, Anxiety, Bipolar Disorder, Schizophrenia, and other neuropsychiatric disorders for over 30 years.

Principal Investigator

Jose Gamez, MD

Principal Investigator, Medical Director & CEO

Dr. Gamez has been practicing medicine for over 34 years in the Miami area. His specialty is psychiatry with a sub-specialty in Geriatric psychiatry. He is the Chief of Psychiatry Department and Medical Director for the psychiatry unit at Palmetto General Medical Center, an Assistant Professor at the University of Miami and Clinical Psychiatry Instructor at Nova Southeastern University. Dr. Gamez is also a community leader and spokesman for Mental Health Topics at Univision Spanish Network and serves as Medical Director and CEO of Galiz Research. Daravan Rath

Site Director

Patrick Yassini, MD

Medical Director & Principal Investigator

Dr. Patrick Yassini serves as the Principal Investigator and Medical Director at Apex Clinical Research, where he brings over 25 years of clinical experience and a strong track record in clinical trial execution. As a dual board-certified physician in family medicine and integrative holistic medicine, Dr. Yassini offers a unique blend of traditional clinical care and forward-thinking research leadership.

Dr. Yassini's experience spans multiple high-enrolling studies across therapeutic areas such as vaccines, obesity, respiratory, neurology, and dermatology. He is deeply involved in protocol execution, subject safety, and data quality, maintaining a hands-on presence throughout the lifecycle of each study. Widely respected in the clinical research and medical communities, Dr. Yassini is known for his integrity, consistency, and results-driven leadership.
