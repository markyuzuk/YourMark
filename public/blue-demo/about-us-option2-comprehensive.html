What is Sensorium?

The Biology

In biology, the sensorium is where all five senses come together to create one complete perception of the world. Your eyes see color, your ears hear sound, your fingers feel texture—but it's only when these senses combine that you truly understand what you're experiencing.

The Metaphor

Each clinical research site has its own local culture, shaped by the community it serves. One site may excel at building trust in a Hispanic neighborhood. Another may understand urban and rural perspectives. Together, they create complete understanding across real-world populations.

Like the five senses, each site provides a unique perspective. Together, they create insights that no single site could capture alone. That's better science.

The Sensorium Effect

Our blueprint for better science: sophisticated operational backbone while protecting the local culture of each site.

What Sites Get

Sites Keep: Their name, their culture, their staff, clinical decisions, their logo, and their soul.

Sites Get Centralized Functions:

  • Centralized business development and study acquisition
  • Finance, HR, payroll, and compliance support
  • Technology systems (CTMS, eReg, CRM)
  • Patient recruitment and marketing support
  • Regulatory operations and QA oversight

What Patients Get

  • Access to cutting-edge treatments
  • Local care from trusted community physicians who understand your culture
  • Dedicated local staff support throughout your journey
  • Contribution to medical breakthroughs
  • Personalized attention and monitoring

What Sponsors Get

  • Faster study startup through centralized operations
  • Superior enrollment driven by trusted local relationships
  • Consistent data quality from operational excellence
  • Diverse patient populations through deep community connections
  • Better retention rates from local culture and patient trust
  • Single point of contact for network

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To prove that culture drives performance in clinical research. We unite independent sites into a network that provides operational strength while preserving what makes each site unique: their local relationships, their community identity, and their commitment to patients.

Our Vision

An industry where clinical trial sites can grow without sacrifice. Where the people who work in research come home fulfilled, not depleted. Where patients trust their local site because the culture that earned that trust remains intact.

Our Core Values

Caring & Happiness

We believe happy people create better outcomes. From our team members to patients to site partners, we prioritize wellbeing, fulfillment, and joy in everything we do.

Local Culture

We are an investigator-led network. Each site keeps its name, its relationships, and its local flavor. Clinical research is inherently local—we protect what makes each site special.

Community Trust

Trust is built in the neighborhood, not just the exam room. We show up for our communities through evidence-based engagement—before, during, and after clinical trials.

Clinical Excellence

We deliver rigorous science without sacrificing the human touch. Great culture and great science aren't opposites—they're partners in achieving better outcomes.

Leadership & Team

Matt Kiernan

Founder & CEO

Sensorium Clinical Research

Matt Kiernan is a life sciences entrepreneur who believes culture is the foundation of great companies. Over the past two decades, he has built and exited two successful organizations in the pharmaceutical industry—Pharmica Consulting and Intrinsic Clinical Systems—both acquired by ProPharma Group in 2021.

Matt's approach blends operational rigor with cultural intention. At Pharmica, he launched a company-wide program built around the philosophy "Work hard, have fun, be great," inspiring consultants, clients, and partners to uphold a standard of mutual respect and service. At Intrinsic, he applied that same mindset to technology, creating tools that helped clinical teams work smarter and stay connected.

Now, he's applying that same philosophy to transform clinical research—building a network where investigators, patients, sponsors, and staff all feel supported and valued.

Education & Credentials: MBA in Finance from Villanova University, dual undergraduate degrees in Business and Psychology from the University of Pittsburgh, certifications in Project Management and Six Sigma Black Belt.
